## Bridge basics

| Term | Abbr. | Definition | Notes | Japanese |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Contract Bridge | — | A card game in which four players form two partnerships, set a contract in the auction, and compete to make or defeat it in the card play. | — | コントラクトブリッジ |
| North / East / South / West | N / E / S / W | The four seats at the table. | — | ノース / イースト / サウス / ウェスト |
| NS / EW | — | The North–South partnership and the East–West partnership. | — | 南北ペア / 東西ペア |
| Partner | — | The seat opposite you in the same partnership. | — | パートナー |
| Opponent | Opp | A seat in the other partnership. | — | 対戦相手 |
| Deal | — | A distribution of the 52 cards, 13 to each seat. | Distinct from a board. | 配札 |
| Board | Bd | One round from the auction through the play, with one deal, one dealer and one vulnerability. | Includes the dealer and vulnerability, not only the deal. | 局 |
| Hand | — | The 13 cards dealt to one seat. | — | 手札 |
| Dealer | — | The seat that calls first in the auction. | — | ディーラー |
| Vulnerability | Vul | A partnership's condition on a board that affects some bonuses for making a contract and penalties for going down. | — | バルネラビリティ |
| Non-vulnerable | Non-vul | The condition of not being vulnerable. | — | ノンバル |
| Trick | — | One card played by each of the four seats. The seat that plays the highest card wins it. | — | トリック |
| Contract | — | The level and denomination (a suit or NT) settled in the auction, together with any Double or Redouble. | The number of tricks needed is the level plus six. | 契約 |
| Declarer | — | The player of the contracting side who also plays dummy's cards. | — | ディクレアラー |
| Dummy | — | Declarer's partner. Dummy's hand is laid face up, and declarer plays its cards. | — | ダミー |
| Defender | — | Each of the two seats trying to defeat the contract. | — | ディフェンダー |
| Opening lead | — | The first card played. The seat to declarer's left leads it. | — | オープニングリード |
| Lead | — | Playing the first card to a trick. | — | リード |
| Trump | — | The suit named in the contract that ranks above the other suits. | — | 切り札 |
| No Trump | NT | A contract with no trump suit. | — | ノートランプ |
| Suit | ♠♥♦♣ | The four kinds of cards. | — | スート |
| Major | M | Spades and hearts. | — | メジャー |
| Minor | m | Diamonds and clubs. | — | マイナー |
| High Card Points | HCP | A count of high cards: A = 4, K = 3, Q = 2, J = 1. | The game and the system use HCP. | ハイカードポイント |
| Made | — | Taking at least the number of tricks the contract needs. | — | 成立 |
| Down | — | Taking fewer tricks than the contract needs. | — | 不成立 |
| Overtrick | — | A trick taken beyond the number the contract needs. | — | オーバートリック |
| Undertrick | — | Each trick short of the number the contract needs. | — | アンダートリック |
| Passed out | — | A board on which all four players pass without any bid, so no contract is set and there is no play. | — | パスアウト |

## Auction and bidding

| Term | Abbr. | Definition | Notes | Japanese |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Auction | — | The phase in which players call in turn, starting with the dealer, to set the contract. | — | オークション |
| Call | — | One action in the auction: a bid, Pass, Double or Redouble. | — | コール |
| Bid | — | A call that names a level and a suit (or NT). | — | ビッド |
| Pass | — | A call that does not bid and passes the turn. | Shown as "Pass" in the game. | パス |
| Double | X | A call on an opponent's bid that increases the penalty if it fails and the score if it makes. | Shown as X in the call history. | ダブル |
| Redouble | XX | A call by the doubled side that increases the score further. | Shown as XX in the call history. | リダブル |
| Opening | — | The first bid in the auction. | — | オープニング |
| Opener | — | The seat that makes the first bid. | See the related documents for the exact conditions. | オープナー |
| Responder | — | Opener's partner. | See the related documents for the exact conditions. | 応答者 |
| Response | — | Partner's first call after the opening. | See the related documents for the exact conditions. | 応答 |
| Rebid | — | The second or later bid by the same seat. | See the related documents for the exact conditions. | 再ビッド |
| Natural | — | A bid that suggests the named suit or NT as a possible contract. | — | 自然 |
| Artificial | — | A bid with an agreed meaning other than the natural meaning of the named suit or NT. | For the exact conditions in the Ichigaya Bidding System, see the [Full reference](bidding-reference.md). | 人工 |
| Non-forcing | NF | A call that creates no new obligation to respond. If no other obligation remains, partner may pass. | NF does not cancel existing obligations. | ノンフォーシング |
| Invitation / Invitational | INV | An invitation to game or slam. Partner may decline. | — | 招待 |
| Forcing for one round | F1 | A call that asks partner for one valid response. | — | ワンラウンド・フォーシング |
| Game-forcing | GF | A state in which the partnership may not stop with a pass until it reaches game. It is not an obligation to reach slam. | — | ゲームフォーシング |
| To play | TO PLAY | A natural call that suggests stopping in that contract. It creates no new obligation. | — | ここで止める提案 |
| Obligation | — | A response the system requires of partner (for example, not passing, or answering an ask). | — | 義務 |
| Obligation to answer | — | The obligation to give the specified answer to an ask such as Gerber, Blackwood or the Refrain. | — | 質問への回答義務 |
| Obligation breach | — | Acting against an obligation the system requires. | The game explains a pass that breaches an obligation. | 義務違反 |
| Trump agreement | — | A publicly agreed trump suit for the rest of the search for a contract. Separate from GF. | — | 切り札合意 |
| Fit | — | The partnership's combined length in a suit (for example, an 8-card fit). | — | フィット |
| Stopper | — | A holding that can stop the opponents' suit in an NT contract. | In the guarantees shown by Bridge Atelier, this means A or KQ. | ストッパー |
| Balanced | — | A distribution with no very short suit (4333, 4432 or 5332). | — | バランス型 |
| Passed hand | — | The hand of a seat that has already passed. | — | パストハンド |
| Jump | — | A bid at a higher level than necessary. | — | ジャンプ |
| Intervention | — | A bid or Double by the opponents in the middle of your side's sequence. | — | 介入 |
| Off-system call | — | A human call that does not follow the system but is legal under the rules. | The Full reference calls these off-system bids or passes. | 規約外のコール |
| Partscore / Game / Slam | — | The size classes of contracts. | — | パートスコア / ゲーム / スラム |
| Penalty Double | — | A Double aimed at defeating the opponents' contract and scoring the penalty. | The Ichigaya Bidding System defines the meaning only for a Double of the opponents' natural suit contract at the 4 level or higher. Takeout Doubles are undefined. | ペナルティDouble |
| Overcall | — | Your side's first bid after the opponents' opening. | See the related documents for the exact conditions. | オーバーコール |

## Ichigaya Bidding System

| Term | Abbr. | Definition | Notes | Japanese |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ichigaya Bidding System | — | The bidding system created for Bridge Atelier. The CPU follows it, and the hints explain it. | The official public name. "This game's system" on screen refers to the same system. | 市ヶ谷ビディング規約 |
| System version | — | The version number of the system. | Currently Ichigaya-v1.1.4 (Bridge Atelier 1.14.6). Separate from the game version and the AI version. | 規約版 |
| Guarantee | GUARANTEE | Lengths, HCP, specific cards or obligations that the system fixes. Later decisions build on them. | — | 保証 |
| Typical range | — | The usual HCP, typical distributions and practice. Not strict limits. | Not a guarantee. The system treats it separately from the CPU estimate, but the game currently shows them together. | 通常目安 |
| CPU estimate | CPU_ESTIMATE | The strength, contract quality and candidate scores that the CPU estimates from its own hand and the public auction. | Separate from the typical range in the system. The game currently shows them together. | AIの推定 |
| Meaning status | DEFINED / PARTIAL / UNDEFINED / CONFLICT / UNSUPPORTED | A classification of whether the meaning of a call in the system is defined, partly defined, undefined, conflicting, or not supported by the CPU. | — | 意味の状態 |
| Ichigaya Refrain | AF01 | An artificial ask that starts with a 4♣ opening. It asks for the number of aces, and, as needed, the number of kings and queens. | It asks for the numbers of A, K and Q, not their locations. | 市ヶ谷リフレイン |
| Ichigaya Fever | — | A nickname for the state when the Ichigaya Refrain is in use. | The official name of the convention is the Ichigaya Refrain. | 市ヶ谷フィーバー |
| Stayman | ST01 | An artificial 2♣ over 1NT that asks opener whether they hold a 4-card major. | Adopted in the Ichigaya Bidding System. | ステイマン |
| Jacoby 2NT | D09 | A direct 2NT over a natural 1♥ / 1♠. An artificial GF that strictly guarantees 4+ support and agrees that major as trumps. | The continuations are defined in the system but not supported by the CPU. The CPU does not start Jacoby 2NT. | ジャコビー2NT |
| Gerber | — | A direct 4♣ over a natural 1NT / 2NT opening. It asks for the number of aces, and a following 5♣ asks for kings. | No queen ask. For CPU support, see the [Bidding System](bidding-system.md). | ガーバー |
| Plain Blackwood | — | 4NT after a trump agreement. It asks for the number of aces, and a following 5NT asks for the number of kings. | No RKCB and no queen ask. For CPU support, see the [Bidding System](bidding-system.md). | プレーン・ブラックウッド |
| Quantitative 4NT | — | 4NT with no trump agreement when a natural NT strength is known. An invitation to 6NT (NF). | Defined in the system but not supported by the CPU. | 量的4NT |
| Artificial fourth suit forcing | FSF | An artificial bid of the fourth suit, used only in four specified sequences (A–D). It asks partner for one answer. Not GF. | Only in the four specified sequences. Not GF. | 人工第四スート・フォーシング |
| Reverse | — | A natural rebid by opener that shows a new suit, higher than the first suit, at the 2 level. The second suit has 4+ cards and the first suit is longer. F1. | — | リバース |
| 2/1 / two-step obligation | R01 / R02 | A non-jump new-suit response at the 2 level to a one-of-a-suit opening (2/1), and the obligations that come with it: one rebid by opener and, below game, one rebid by responder. 2/1 is not GF. | 1♣–2♦ is a jump new suit, so it is GF, not 2/1. | 2/1と二段階義務 |
| Strong natural two-bid | — | An opening of 2♣ to 2♠. Natural, usually 22+ points, F1 for responder. Not an artificial 2♣. | Different from an artificial 2♣ opening. | 強い自然2 |
| Weak three-level opening (preempt) | — | 3♣ to 3♠ as the first bid. Natural and NF, guaranteeing 6+ cards. | The system label is PREEMPT3. The game shows its meaning as "Natural 3-of-a-suit opening". | 弱い3スート開始 |
| Special 4M opening | D04 | A natural 4♥ / 4♠ opening as the first bid. It shows a long, strong major that can expect game without help from partner (game on its own). | Not a weak preempt. There is no public guarantee of length, HCP or playing tricks. | 特殊4M |
| Simple raise | — | A natural raise of partner's suit by one level. | — | 単純支持 |
| Conventions not adopted | — | Existing conventions that the Ichigaya Bidding System does not use. | Transfers, RKCB, queen asks in Gerber and Plain Blackwood, cue bids, and others. The Ichigaya Refrain does have a queen ask. Distinct from undefined conventions. | 不採用の取り決め |

## Bridge Atelier

| Term | Abbr. | Definition | Notes | Japanese |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bridge Atelier | — | The name of the product. | The official product name. | Bridge Atelier |
| Browser version | — | The version you can play in a browser. A few features, such as saving decision logs and opening deal files, are available only in the Windows version. | Provided on itch.io. The Windows version is distributed on GitHub Releases. Technical names, such as distribution file names, call it WebGL. | Web版 |
| Title screen | — | The screen shown at start-up. Choose the number of boards to start a set, or switch the language. | — | タイトル画面 |
| Set | — | A unit of 4, 8 or 16 boards played together. The board scores are added up. | — | セット |
| Number of boards | — | The number of boards in a set: 4, 8 or 16. | — | 局数 |
| Set seed | — | An integer that determines the deal seed and AI seed of every board in a set. Left blank, it is chosen automatically. | Within the same game version, the same set seed gives the same deals from the start, whatever the number of boards. | セットSEED |
| Deal seed | — | An integer that determines the deal of one board. | Automatically generated values and manually entered values have different allowed ranges. For a single deal it also determines the dealer and vulnerability; in a set, those follow the board number. | 配札SEED |
| AI seed / AI decision seed | — | An integer that determines the random choices in the AI's calls and plays. | Used by both the bidding AI and the card-play AI. The game shows "AI seed" in some places. | AI判断SEED |
| Mode | — | Whether a human sits South or four AI players play automatically. | — | モード |
| AI table | — | A second table where four AI players play the same deal with the same AI seed. Your IMP comparison is against it. | Not a human duplicate competition. | AI卓 |
| AI | — | Seats and decisions controlled by the computer. | Also called CPU. Some on-screen labels use CPU. | AI |
| Auction hints | — | A feature in human-plus-three-AI mode that shows the system meaning of calls (and, if selected, an AI suggestion) in the center of the table on your auction turns. Three settings. | Off / Show system meanings (default) / Meanings + AI suggestion. | オークションのヒント |
| AI suggestion | — | On your turn, the call the same AI would choose, for reference. Not necessarily the best call. | Not guaranteed to be optimal. "Recommendation" still appears in some English screen text. | AIの推奨 |
| Review | — | The screen for going back over each decision in the auction and play after a board ends. | — | 振り返り |
| Decision review | — | The area in review that shows the meaning, reasoning, candidates and scores for the selected call or card. | AI suggestions are available in review even if auction hints were off. | 判断の確認 |
| Candidates compared and scores | — | The calls the AI compared and their internal scores. Not winning chances. | The scores are not winning chances. | 比較した候補と評価値 |
| Decision log | — | A research record, in the Windows version, that automatically saves the AI's and the human's decisions for every board. | Not saved in the browser version. Decisions at the AI table are not saved. | 判断ログ |
| Export text | — | A provisional feature for checking that writes the review out as text. | A provisional feature for checking. How it is handled in the public release is undecided. | テキスト出力 |
| AI thinking level | — | How much the CPU computes when choosing a card: Fast, Normal or Thorough. | Shown on screen as "CPU thinking level". Separate from the pace. | AIの思考レベル |
| Pace | — | How long calls and cards stay visible. It does not change the CPU's decisions. | Adjusts on-screen waiting time without changing what the AI decides. | 進行の速さ |
| Data folder | — | The folder that holds settings, saved deals, decision logs and review text. | A Windows feature. In the browser version, files are received as downloads. | データフォルダー |

## Scoring

| Term | Abbr. | Definition | Notes | Japanese |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Score | — | The points for the result of one board. On each board, only one partnership scores. | Records and IMP comparisons use a signed value from NS's point of view. | 得点 |
| Duplicate bridge scoring table | — | A scoring table that scores each board independently (Laws of Duplicate Bridge, Law 77). | Scoring based on Law 77. It does not mean a duplicate competition is held. | デュプリケートブリッジの得点表 |
| Trick score | — | The basic points for the tricks contracted for (from the seventh trick up to the contract). | Overtricks are counted separately. | トリック点 |
| Game / part-score / slam / doubled / redoubled bonus | — | Points added according to the size of the contract or a Double. | — | ボーナス（ゲーム・パートスコア・スラム・ダブル成立・リダブル成立） |
| Undertrick penalty | — | Points scored by the opponents for each trick the contract falls short. | — | アンダートリックの罰点 |
| Board number | — | The number of a board. It determines the dealer and vulnerability (Law 2). | In a set, it determines the dealer and vulnerability. | ボード番号 |
| Total points | — | The board scores of a set added up for NS and EW (Law 78C). Strongly affected by the strength of the hands. | — | 合計点 |
| International Match Points | IMP | A unit that converts the score difference between two tables using a fixed scale. | Shown as IMP in the game. Used for the comparison with the AI table. | インターナショナル・マッチポイント |
| Versus AI table | — | The difference between this table's NS score and the AI table's NS score, converted to IMPs. Only in human-plus-three-AI sets. | An IMP comparison with the result of four AI players playing the same deal. Not a ranking among human players. | AI卓との比較 |
| Set results | — | A list of each board's result in the set, the totals, and the comparison with the AI table. | — | セット成績 |
| Duplicate bridge competition | — | A form of competition in which the same boards are played at several tables and the results compared. | Bridge Atelier does not reproduce competition or ranking among human players. | デュプリケート競技 |

## Notes

- **System definitions and AI support:** A convention being defined in the system does not mean the AI can carry out all of it. For details, see the [Bidding System](bidding-system.md).
- **Reproducibility:** Reproducing deals with seeds is limited to the same game version. The card-play AI's decisions also depend on the thinking level (the bidding AI's decisions do not). Human calls and cards also change what follows.
- **Scoring:** The game uses the duplicate bridge scoring table, but it does not rank human players as in a duplicate competition.
