# Getting Started — Bridge Atelier

[Back to README](../../README.md) · [日本語](../ja/getting-started.md) · [User Manual](user-manual.md)

This is the shortest route to your first set in Bridge Atelier. For controls and scoring details, see the [User Manual](user-manual.md).

## 1. Launch the game

- **Windows:** Get the release from GitHub Releases and launch it according to the included instructions.
- **Browser version:** Open the published page in your browser. A few features are available only in the Windows version.

Find the distribution links in the [README](../../README.md).

## 2. Set up a game on the title screen

1. Choose your language on the title screen if needed. Language selection is available there.
2. Select **4, 8, or 16 boards**. Four boards are a good starting point.
3. You can leave the **set seed** blank to generate one automatically. Within the same game version, the same set seed produces the same initial deals regardless of set length.
4. Choose the mode where you play **South**, then start the set. You can also watch a four-AI table.

## 3. Bid in the auction

Choose a call on your turn. The center of South's nameplate shows the current status. By default, auction hints **show system meanings**. The other settings are **Off** and **Meanings + AI suggestion**. AI suggestions are guidance, not guaranteed optimal calls.

When the contract is set, an "Auction over" panel appears in the center. Check the contract, dummy, and opening leader, then press [Start play ▶] (Enter on the keyboard). If all four players pass (a passed-out board), the result appears without confirmation.

## 4. Play the cards

Card play begins when you press [Start play ▶]. Select cards to play according to the auction result. If you are declarer, you also choose the cards from dummy. If you are defending, you play alongside an AI partner. See the [User Manual](user-manual.md) for the options when the human player is dummy.

## 5. Review the result

After each board, review the auction, card play, and score. In human-plus-three-AI sets, the game also compares your result against a **four-AI table** using IMPs. Continue through the remaining boards to finish the set.
